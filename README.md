## 🎓 Teaching — IE University

Adjunct Professor in the Dual Degree in Business Administration & Data and Business Analytics.
Course materials are public and taught in R on the tidyverts stack, following
[*Forecasting: Principles and Practice*, 3rd ed.](https://otexts.com/fpp3/)

| Course | When | Repo |
|---|---|---|
| Time Series Analysis | Semester 1, Fall 2026 · 20 sessions | [tsa-fpp3-2026](https://github.com/aleberriz/tsa-fpp3-2026) |
| Forecasting for Time Series | Semester 2, from Jan 2027 · 15 sessions | *in preparation* |

The method is **build it by hand, then verify against the library.** Students implement classical
decomposition, simple exponential smoothing, prediction intervals and the error metrics themselves
before calling the function that does them.
